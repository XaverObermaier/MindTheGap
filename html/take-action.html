News Check

Paste a news article or claim below. This runs entirely in your browser and checks for common misinformation patterns — it does not verify facts, and a full AI-assisted version would need a backend we don't have on this static site yet.

    Won't: declare a claim true or false. It only flags patterns — you still decide, and can check the primary source.
